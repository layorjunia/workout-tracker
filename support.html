Workout Tracker

Questions, bugs or ideas? Email jacob@illuminatedrones.com and include your iPhone model and iOS version.

How do I delete my account?

Open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account, then enter your PIN. Your account and its synced data are permanently deleted.

My steps are not showing.

Open the iPhone Settings app → Health → Data Access & Devices → Workout Tracker, and make sure Steps is on. Then reopen the app.

How do I move my workouts to a new phone?

Sign in with the same name and PIN on the new phone and your data syncs down. You can also export a backup from Settings → Backup.

I forgot my PIN.

Email us from the address you used and we can delete the account so you can start a new one. Your workouts stay on your phone until you sign out.

Not medical advice

Workout Tracker is a general fitness log. Its numbers and goals are not medical advice. Talk to a doctor before changing your exercise or diet.
