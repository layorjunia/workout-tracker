Privacy Policy

Sets & Steps · Effective November 3, 2026

Sets & Steps (“the app”) is published by Illuminate Drones, LLC (“we”). This page explains what the app does with your information.

The short version. Your workouts and health numbers stay on your iPhone unless you choose to sign in. If you sign in, a copy syncs to your account so it is on all your devices. We do not sell your data, show ads, or track you across other apps and websites.

What the app keeps on your device

Workouts, sets, exercises and templates; goals and tallies; nutrition entries; body weight; profile details you enter (height, weight, age, sex, activity level); settings; and daily health summaries.

Apple Health

If you allow it, the iPhone app reads these from Apple Health: steps, heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, sleep, active energy, body weight, body fat, workouts, and dietary energy, protein, carbohydrates and fat. It uses them only to show your steps, streaks, trends and goals inside the app and its Home Screen widget. The app never writes to Apple Health. Health data is not used for advertising, marketing or data mining, and is not shared with anyone for those purposes. You can change what the app may read at any time in Settings → Health → Data Access & Devices.

Signing in (optional)

You can use the app without an account. If you sign in with a name (or email) and a 4-digit PIN, the app creates an account with Google Firebase Authentication. When you enter a name instead of an email, the app turns it into a placeholder address ending in @workout-tracker.local so the account has a unique ID. Your PIN is used as your password; Firebase stores only a protected form of it.

A copy of your app data, everything listed above including the daily health summaries the app has saved, is stored in Google Cloud Firestore under your account so it syncs between your devices. Data is encrypted in transit, and Google encrypts stored data at rest.

Who else handles data

  • Google (Firebase) stores account and synced data on our behalf.
  • Vercel hosts the app’s pages. Like any web host, it sees technical request information, such as your IP address, when the app loads.

The app contains no advertising, analytics or tracking services.

Deleting your data

Open Settings → Cloud sync → Delete account and enter your PIN. This permanently deletes your account and the synced copy of your data, and clears the app’s data on that device. Signing out also clears the app’s data on that device, but your synced copy stays in your account until you delete it. Deleting the app removes its data from your iPhone. You can also email us and we will delete your account for you.

Children

The app is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their information.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
